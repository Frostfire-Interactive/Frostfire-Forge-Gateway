An account can have several characters. Each has its own name, class, level, items, spells, quests and gold. Your friends, mounts and account settings belong to the account.

## Your character list

After you enter a realm you see **Your characters**. Each row shows a character's name, level and class. Select a character to see its class on the right, then select **Play** (or press **Enter**, or double-click the row) to go into the game with it.

**Back** returns to realm selection. To switch to another character while in the game, open the menu and select **Change character**.

Only one character of an account is in the game at a time.

## Making a character

Select **Create character**. Choose a class on the left, type a name at the foot of the screen, and select **Create**. The new character appears in your list, ready to play.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Name | 3 to 15 letters, numbers or underscores. No other character, and no other account, can already have it. It cannot start with "guest_". |
| Number of characters | Up to 5 per account (the realm can set another number). **Create character** is hidden when you have the most you can have. |

An account with no characters opens straight on making one.

## Deleting a character

Select the character, then **Delete**. Type its name to confirm and select **Delete** again.

- The character leaves your list right away and is removed for good after 7 days. Its name stays taken until then.
- Characters cannot be deleted while you are in the game. Leave the game first.
- A guild leader with other members in the guild must pass on leadership first.

## Guests

A guest has one character, named after the guest account. On the character page a guest only chooses its class, and then goes into the game.
