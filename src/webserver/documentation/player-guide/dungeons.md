A dungeon is a place that your group plays on its own. When you walk into a dungeon portal, the game opens a private copy of the dungeon for you and your party. Nobody outside your group can enter that copy, and nobody in it is disturbed by anyone else.

## Portals

A dungeon portal is an area on the ground of a map, inside a stone arch. Walk into it from the front, up through the arch's opening, to enter. The portal fires when you step into it from in front, so standing in one does nothing. The arch is solid: you cannot walk through its pillars or in from behind it.

You cannot use a portal while you are dead, a ghost, or in combat. Stop fighting and try again.

When you enter, the game remembers the portal you used. Leaving the dungeon puts you back in the centre of that portal.

Walk up to a portal and its opening shows the other side. A dungeon portal shows the dungeon's entrance, with the players of your own or your party's copy moving there; with no copy open it is a still picture, and other groups' copies are never shown. The exit inside a dungeon shows the spot outside you return to. Some portals are **teleports**: they take you one way to another place, without a copy, and show that place and the players there. Some portals show only their swirl.

After you go through a portal, in or out, you wait **3 seconds** before you can use a portal or a warp to another map again. A portal walked into sooner says "You can warp again in" followed by the seconds left. Step out of it and in again once the time is up.

## Solo, party and raid

| Kind | Who can enter | Group size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dungeon | A solo player, or a party of up to 5 | 5 players |
| Raid | A solo player, or a party up to the size chosen. A party of more than 5 is a raid group. | 10 or 20 players |

- A solo player can enter a dungeon alone. A raid also takes a solo player.
- **The party enters together, in one copy.** The first member through the portal opens it. The others who go through after are brought into the same copy while it has room, even if they still have a copy of their own open.
- In a **raid**, a party member who is not the leader cannot open it: "Your party leader must open this raid." Once the leader has opened it, the others can join.
- A group larger than the dungeon's limit is refused: "Your group is too large for this dungeon (limit 5)." A raid at size 10 refuses a group of more than 10, and at size 20 a group of more than 20.
- A copy that already holds as many players as its size refuses more: "This dungeon is full."
- A dungeon that is switched off says "This dungeon is closed." If you were already inside it, the portal still takes you back in.

A raid group is a party converted past 5 members. See [Parties](#/player-guide/parties/raid-groups).

## Modes

Some raids can be opened at 10 players, at 20 players, or both. When there is a choice, a window named after the raid opens with the text "Choose a group size" and one button for each size, plus **Cancel**. Pick a size within 60 seconds, or the choice is dropped and you walk into the portal again.

The size also sets how hard the dungeon is. Creatures in a larger mode have more health and hit harder: by default 10 players doubles their health and raises their damage by half, and 20 players quadruples their health and doubles their damage. The people who built the dungeon can change these numbers.

## The entry lockout

Every character has 10 dungeon entries each hour. Opening a copy or joining one that is already open uses one. The hour starts at your first entry and runs for 60 minutes. Then the count starts again.

- **Going back into a copy you are already in costs nothing.** Leaving and returning, losing your connection, or walking out through a portal and in again are all free while the copy is open.
- When you have used all 10, a portal says "You have used all 10 dungeon entries this hour. Next entry in" followed by the minutes to wait.

## What is inside

- Each copy has its own creatures. They stand where the dungeon's map places them, at full strength for the mode you chose.
- Chests in a copy do not refill while the copy is open. A new copy has them full again.
- The weather in a copy is the weather of the dungeon's map. It stays the same from level to level and while you are inside, and it changes when the map's weather changes.
- When a copy closes, its creatures, chests and loot on the ground are removed.
- Some creatures are bosses. When the last boss of a copy dies, the copy is cleared and its chests appear. A copy with no boss has its chests from the start.

## Personal loot

Inside a dungeon every member of the group who took part in a kill, and who is near the creature, gets **a roll of their own** on the creature's loot. You do not share a body's items, and there is no turn order. Each player looting sees only their own items.

The creature's coins are rolled once and split between the same players. Experience and quest credit are shared as in [Parties](#/player-guide/parties).

## Death

When you die inside a dungeon, you stay where you fell. You can **release your spirit** at any time:

- You come back to life at once with full health and stamina at the dungeon's entrance. There is no ghost run and no graveyard.
- If the dungeon has since been removed, you are sent to your portal outside instead.

A dead player cannot use a portal.

## Leaving

You leave a dungeon by:

- walking into a dungeon portal inside it, which takes you to the portal you came in through,
- being put out by the game, as described below.

When the last player leaves, the copy stays open for **5 minutes** so you can come back. After that it closes. A cleared copy closes as soon as it is empty.

If you log out inside a dungeon and its copy is still open and you were part of it, you log in inside it again. Otherwise you log in at your portal, or at the realm's starting point if you never entered one.

### Staying with your group

If you are in a copy with other players and no one else in it is in your party, the game puts you out after **60 seconds**. A player alone in a copy is never put out, and neither is a player who is dead or a ghost.

This keeps strangers out of a private copy. If your party breaks up, stay with someone from it, or leave.

### Editing

Editing, saving and opening the map editor are refused inside a dungeon, with the notice "Leave the dungeon to edit its map".

## Tips

:::tip Count your entries
Ten entries an hour is generous for a group, but quick retries add up. A full party that fails a boss can wait in the copy rather than leave and come back: the copy stays open while someone is in it.
:::

:::tip Form the party first
Invite your group before you walk in. A player who is not in your party opens a copy of their own, and is put out of yours after a minute.
:::
