Your account holds everything you earn in the game, so it is worth a few minutes to protect it. Everything on this page happens on the **Profile** page.

## Open your profile

There are two ways in:

- On the home page, select **Manage profile**.
- In the game, press **Esc** and select **Manage Profile**. It opens in a new tab.

The top of the page shows your **Username**, your **Email** and your **Last Login**. Your email is hidden. Select the reveal button next to it and enter your password to see it.

:::note
If none of the sign-in protections are active, the page shows a warning that your account is not protected by two-factor authentication.
:::

## Confirming a change

Every change to your account's security is confirmed with the strongest two-factor method you have set up:

| You have set up | You confirm with |
|-----------------|------------------|
| A security key | A tap on one of your keys |
| An authenticator app, but no key | The code from the app |
| Neither | A code sent to your email |

This applies to changing your email or password, resetting a forgotten password, adding or removing an authenticator app or a security key, and turning a **Required at sign-in** switch off. Turning a switch on needs no confirmation. The method counts as soon as it is set up, whether or not its switch is on.

:::warning Keep a spare
Removing a security key needs a tap on a key, and removing an authenticator app needs a key or the app. Register a second key, and keep your authenticator app backed up, so that losing one does not lock these changes.
:::

## Change your email

1. Under **Email Address**, type the new address into **New Email**.
2. Select **Update Email**.
3. Enter your account password. With a security key or an authenticator app set up, confirm with it.
4. A code is sent to your **current** email. Enter it to continue.
5. A second code is sent to your **new** email. Enter it to confirm.

The change only happens once both codes are confirmed. An address that another account already uses is refused.

## Change your password

1. Under **Password**, enter your **Current Password**.
2. Enter the new password twice, in **New Password** and **Confirm Password**.
3. Select **Update Password**.
4. Confirm the change with your strongest method (see [Confirming a change](#/player-guide/account-security/confirming-a-change)).

The new password must be 8 to 20 characters long, with at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one special character.

:::tip Let the game pick one
Select **Generate** next to the new password field to get a strong random password. Save it in a password manager.
:::

## Two-factor sign-in

Two-factor sign-in means a password alone is not enough to get into your account. There are three methods, and you can use more than one.

| Method | What you need | How it works at login |
|--------|---------------|-----------------------|
| Email codes | Access to your email | A code is emailed to you after you enter your password. |
| Authenticator app | An authenticator app on your phone | You type the 6 digit code the app shows. |
| Security key | A FIDO2 security key | You insert the key and tap it when the browser asks. |

Each method has a **Required at sign-in** switch on the profile page. A method only protects your logins while its switch is on.

### Email codes

New accounts start with this switched on. Use the switch in the **Email Address** section to turn it on or off.

### Authenticator app

1. Under **Authenticator App**, select **Set Up**.
2. Enter your account password and select **Continue**, then confirm with your strongest method.
3. Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
4. Type the 6 digit code from the app and select **Verify & Enable**.

Then turn on **Required at sign-in** in the same section so the app is asked for at login.

To remove the app from your account, select **Disable**, enter your password and confirm with your strongest method.

### Security keys

1. Under **Security Keys**, select **Add Key**.
2. Enter your account password and select **Continue**, then confirm with your strongest method.
3. Give the key a name you will recognise, then select **Register Key**.
4. Follow your browser's prompt and tap your key.

Your keys are listed in the same section. You can register more than one, which is a good idea in case you lose one. Turn on **Required at sign-in** to use your keys at login.

To remove a key, select its remove button, enter your password and tap one of your keys.

### Signing in with two-factor

1. Enter your username and password as usual.
2. If email codes are on, enter the code from your email.
3. If an authenticator app or security key is required, the **Two-Factor Authentication** page opens. With both required, you choose between **Verify with Security Key** and **Use Authenticator App**.

:::warning Keep a way back in
Before you rely on an authenticator app or a security key, make sure you can still reach the email address on your account. A forgotten password is reset through your email, confirmed with your strongest method.
:::

## Reset a forgotten password

1. On the home page, select **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your account's email address and select **Send Reset Link**.
3. Open the email and select **Reset Password**.
4. Enter a new password twice. With a security key or an authenticator app set up, confirm with it.
5. Select **Reset Password**. You are sent back to the login screen.

For privacy, the page shows the same message whether or not the email belongs to an account. If no email arrives, check the address and your spam folder.

:::note
A reset link works once. If you need another try, request a new link.
:::

## Good habits

- Use a password you do not use anywhere else.
- Turn on at least one two-factor method and switch on **Required at sign-in** for it.
- Never share verification codes with anyone. Nobody needs them except you.
- Select **Logout** from the menu when you finish playing on a shared computer.
